# The prompt

Ninth game in the harsh-critic-loop series. Levels, gods, duels, and
descents became theorems; now whole careers go under oath: **voyages
as theorems.**

```
Build an Elite tribute at the level of a modern commercial minimalist
space sim. Single-file browser game, zero assets. Original hulls and
worlds — the real Elite (Braben & Bell, 1984) is copyrighted. Wireframe
3D on canvas, pitch-and-roll-only flight, a seeded galaxy of typed
economies, the slot on the spinning station that must be met roll-for-
roll, witch-space between the stars, and the ladder from Harmless to
Elite.

One owner writes the whole game. Then harsh sub-agent critics: the
three visual lenses plus an ELITE-FIDELITY critic judging mechanics
against the 1984 canon.

The harness requirement is absolute: the trader bot must grow 100
credits into wealth by reading the economy — and a blind-cargo control
must die broke, proving the prices are load-bearing; the duel bot must
win where the sitting duck dies; the aligned docker must dock where
the crooked one is ground off the hull; and every law — price spreads,
fuel arithmetic, bounties, ranks, legal status, station rotation, the
pursuit geometry of two axes — gets an isolated machine-checked proof.
Grade against the commercial bar, report the honest number.
```
